title: Historical Accuracy
---

I've used [the timescape wiki](https://timescape.wiki) as the primary resource
for historical lore. Unfortunatly details is pretty sparse and almost all the
references are behind a Patreon paywall which makes it very difficult to learn
try and find more depth.

As such, details may not be entirly accurate or inconsistent with canonical
lore.

I'm not particlarly concerned about that however, since this is really
documenting more of what the Alderley branch _believe_ to be historically
accurate. The Alderley archives cover documents over several millennia, and are
sometimes second order translations where the originals are now indecyperable.
